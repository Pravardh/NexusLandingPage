How it went

The Odyssey was our first proper event. It ran for about six hours, with roughly fifteen teams building something from scratch in a single afternoon. We kept everyone going with pizza and drinks, and by the end every team got up to present what they'd made. The ideas were sharper and more varied than we expected for a first event. Plenty of people walked in not knowing anyone and left having built a working demo with a team they'd only just met. For a first attempt, we were really happy with how it came together.

What we learned

→

Six hours was about the right length. Most teams used nearly the full window, and nobody felt rushed or ran out of things to do.

→

Pizza and drinks kept the energy up. Next time we'll get the food in a little earlier to smooth over the mid-afternoon dip.

→

Fifteen demos back to back is a lot to sit through. We'll give each team a tighter, clearer slot so the presentations stay sharp.

→

The best part was watching people who had never met team up and actually ship something. That's what we want more of, so we'll help people find teammates earlier next time.

If you were there, thanks for making it what it was. If you missed it, the next one is already in the works.
